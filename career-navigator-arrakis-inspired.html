01 / The challenge

There is no shortage of options. There is a shortage of clarity.

Courses, job titles, advice and trends can pull you in every direction. A useful career decision starts by connecting what matters to you with practical ways to test a path.

03 / The method

From a few answers to a plan you can test.

A good recommendation should not end at a job title. It should help you understand the choice, try the work, and learn from what happens.

A simple framework for making a better-informed decision.

Use each stage to gather evidence about what suits you — and adjust as you learn.

STAGE 01

Understand

Map interests, strengths and current constraints.

STAGE 02

Explore

Compare pathways, daily work and requirements.

STAGE 03

Prepare

Choose skills and resources that fit your situation.

STAGE 04

Experiment

Try a small project, task or conversation.

STAGE 05

Review

Use feedback to continue, adjust or compare.
